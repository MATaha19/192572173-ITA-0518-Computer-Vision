Cause (amber) and effect (red) nodes trace how a deficiency in each axis produces a specific visual artifact; both artifact chains converge on the isopreference trade-off — the classic finding that perceived quality is a joint function of resolution and gray levels, not either in isolation — which then determines perceived quality and, ultimately, real-world usability.
